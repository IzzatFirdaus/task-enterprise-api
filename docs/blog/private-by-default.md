---
title: Private by default: why task ownership matters
description: A practical look at keeping personal work scoped to the person who created it.
date: 2026-09-03
---

Private work should not require a complicated explanation. Enterprise Tasks scopes ordinary task reads and writes to the authenticated owner, keeping the personal workflow simple and the boundary explicit.

The result is a useful default: people can focus on their queue without accidentally exposing another person's work.
